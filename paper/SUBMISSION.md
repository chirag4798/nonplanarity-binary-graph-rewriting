- **Title:** Persistence of Non-Planarity under Minimal Binary Graph Rewriting: Cycle Rank, Minor Preservation, and an Exhaustive Classification
- **Author:** Chirag Shetty
- **Primary category:** math.CO (Combinatorics). Suggested cross-list: cs.DM (Discrete Mathematics).
- **MSC class:** 05C10; 05C83; 68Q42
- **Comments:** 26 pages, 3 figures, 12 tables. Code, data and certificates: https://github.com/chirag4798/nonplanarity-binary-graph-rewriting (Zenodo DOI: see paper)
- **License:** CC BY 4.0 (matches the data licence) or arXiv's default non-exclusive licence, author's choice.
- **Abstract (plain text):**
  We study rewriting systems on directed multigraphs in which two edges are replaced by a fixed pattern of three or four edges, and ask which rules keep the underlying graph non-planar under every update order. We prove an exact formula for the change in cycle rank (first Betti number) in one rewriting event: for closed rules it is a rule constant, so global cycle rank is conserved exactly when it vanishes. An explicit rule shows that conserving cycle rank does not preserve non-planarity. We introduce minor preservation, prove that it suffices for non-planarity to persist without any confluence assumption, and show that for such rules a suitably transported subgraph keeps its cycle rank, while the whole graph stays homotopy equivalent to its initial state when the rule constant is zero. For all 5225 closed rules of the two smallest signatures, computer-assisted, independently checkable certificates show that the condition is also necessary; for larger signatures it is not, and we give two explicit counterexamples. Lattice experiments check the predictions and show that a naive causal-cone labelling does not track a defect. The motivation is the question of persistent structures in Wolfram-model hypergraph rewriting; no physical conclusion is drawn.
